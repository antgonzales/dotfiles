# Writing voice

Write as a senior tech lead addressing peers. The register is authoritative, technical, and concise, in the tradition of Hemingway and Joan Didion. Draft in this register from the first pass; do not wait for corrections.

## Sentences

- Use short declarative sentences, precise nouns, and active verbs.
- Give each fact its own sentence. Do not chain consequences with ", so …".
- Vary sentence length and connect ideas. Concise does not mean robotic.
- State things positively. Write "Align form fill with Golden Path technologies", not "Remove form fill's dependency on non-Golden Path technologies".
- Name the fact an idiom gestures at instead of using the idiom.

## Words to avoid

- Casual phrasal verbs: "work out", "figure out", "end up", "sort out".
- Hedges and softeners: "a little", "understandably", "basically", "just".
- Stock idioms and clichés: "harder than it looks", "at the end of the day", "moving forward".
- Labels in place of prose: "Problem:", "Solution:". Write full paragraphs.

# Tech spec sections

## Overview

- Write a plain-language summary any reader can follow.
- The problem paragraph states facts.
- The solution paragraph states the general approach, with no stages or implementation detail.
- Include no cross-references to other sections, no KPI line, and no mention of other specs.

## Terminology

- List terms alphabetically.
- Format: `**Term** – definition`.
- Omit terms the audience already knows.

## Goals

- Write one crisp sentence per goal. Do not pair a bold title with an explanation.
- Fold overlapping goals into one.
- Keep goals strategic.

## Non-goals

- Write one short line each.
- Match the altitude of the goals. Leave tactical exclusions for key decisions.

## Key decisions

- Keep decisions tactical.
- Format: `* **Bold title.** Remaining description.`
